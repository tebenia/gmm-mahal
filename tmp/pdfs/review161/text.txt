          10. Comments to the authors. Constructive comments are highly appreciated.
                                                                                  
          This manuscript provides a focused analysis of trigger value selection in clean-label
          malware backdoor attacks. Treating trigger values separately from feature selection
                                                                                  
          and poisoning-sample selection is a useful perspective, and the comparison of
          attack effectiveness with sanitizer-based detectability adds practical value beyond
                                                                                  
          an ASR-only evaluation. To further improve the clarity and reproducibility of the
          manuscript, the reviewer suggests addressing the following points.      
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Major Comments                                                          
                                                                                  
          1. The results across random seeds should be reported more explicitly.  
          The manuscript states that additional runs with “several random seeds” were
                                                                                  
          conducted, but the number of runs or seeds is not specified. Please state the
          number of repeated trials and consider reporting aggregate results, such as the
                                                                                  
          mean and standard deviation, to show the stability of the reported trends.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          2. Please provide additional experimental details required for reproducibility.

          10. Comments to the authors. Constructive comments are highly appreciated.
          This work offers an interesting perspective on clean-label backdoor attacks. It treats
                                                                                  
          trigger value selection as an independent design factor. This is an aspect that has
          received comparatively little attention in prior studies, which mainly focused on
                                                                                  
          trigger feature selection. The paper systematically compares several new selection
          strategies against existing ones. The framing around the effectiveness–detectability
                                                                                  
          trade-off is a valuable contribution. It could also inform future defense research.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          I would recommend that the authors consider expanding on the following point to
          further strengthen the paper:                                           
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          Section IV states that attack effectiveness is measured using both "clean accuracy"
                                                                                  
          and "attack success rate (ASR)". However, no clean accuracy values are reported
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  

--- PAGE 3 ---
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          anywhere in the text, tables, or figures. Maintaining performance on clean data is
          central to what makes clean-label attacks distinctive and difficult to notice.
                                                                                  
          Reporting this metric would help substantiate that premise.             
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          In addition, the paper references several other evaluations worth seeing in full.
          These include results at 3% and 5% poison rates, mean and standard deviation
                                                                                  
          values across multiple random seeds, and concrete numbers for HDBSCAN and
          Spectral Signature. All of these sound like interesting additions. However, the
                                                                                  
          corresponding results are not shown in much detail. Given the page constraints of a
          conference paper, I look forward to seeing these results reported at the
                                                                                  
          presentation.

          10. Comments to the authors. Constructive comments are highly appreciated.
          This paper addresses a critical and timely issue: the vulnerability of malware
                                                                                  
          detection systems to "clean-label" backdoor attacks. The authors aptly identify that
          an effective attack is one that not only achieves a high Attack Success Rate (ASR)
                                                                                  
          but also remains stealthy against common sanitization techniques (characterized by
          low "poison recall").                                                   
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          While the paper evaluates "poison recall" using Isolation Forests, it would be
                                                                                  
          beneficial to briefly discuss in the presentation how other types of sanitizers and
          robust learning methods perform against the most effective technique identified
                                                                                  
          (CorrCountAbsSHAP).
